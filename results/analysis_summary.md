# Analysis Summary

## Busiest Stations

The analysis identified the top 10 stations based on daily entries.

## Waiting Time

Average waiting time was compared across different zones to identify
differences in passenger waiting conditions.

## Complaints

Stations with more than 20 monthly complaints and average waiting
time above 3 minutes were identified for further investigation.

## Parking

Stations were classified according to parking availability.

## Delays

Weekday and weekend delays were combined to calculate total weekly
delays for each station.

## Station Ranking

Stations were ranked based on daily passenger entries.

## Exploratory Attention Score

A custom score was calculated by combining the relative rankings of
daily entries, complaints and waiting time.
